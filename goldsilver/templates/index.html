About the ratio

What it measures

The ratio is the number of ounces of silver it takes to buy one ounce of gold. It compares the two metals without reference to any currency.

How it is used

A high reading can suggest silver is cheap relative to gold, and a low reading the opposite. It says nothing about whether either metal is cheap in absolute terms.

Where the data comes from

Prices and exchange rates come from {{ source }}. They are delayed spot references, not live dealing prices, and each reading shows when it was last updated.
